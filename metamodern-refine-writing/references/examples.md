# Examples

These examples show the level of intervention. They are calibration examples, not templates.

## Personal outreach

Before:

> I am not sure if this is even the right ask, but I am looking for difficult brand and product problems where strategy and making are tangled together. I think we are uniquely positioned to help with that work.

After:

> I am not sure if this is even the right ask, but I am looking for difficult brand and product problems where strategy and making are tangled together. I think we are uniquely positioned to help with exactly that kind of work.

The uncertainty and positioning claim stay. The revision connects the broad claim to the concrete work already named.

## Voice that should survive

Before:

> The mess is the work. The brand is lying a little. That is where I get useful.

After: unchanged.

The fragments and unusual phrasing carry the point and the author's voice.

## Client update

Before:

> The deck was still doing that AI-deck thing where every slide lands with the same weight and confidence. It is not final.

After: unchanged.

The phrase is clear in context, and the uncertainty is material.

## Operational prose

Before:

> It is important to note that we have made meaningful progress on the cache work. We have not verified the invalidation path.

After:

> We have made meaningful progress on the cache work. We have not verified the invalidation path.

The empty setup is gone. The positive status claim and its degree remain. The uncertainty stays separate and unchanged.

## Already-good writing

> The database stores the row first. Slack and Google Sheets then retry independently.

Return it unchanged. A refinement skill must be able to stop editing.

## Review mode

Draft:

> The new workflow delivers a robust and seamless experience that empowers teams to move faster.

Review:

1. The sentence claims qualities without naming a mechanism or result.
2. Replace it with the measured change, failure removed, or action now possible.
3. If the source contains no supporting fact, preserve the author's position in plainer language and flag the evidence gap.
