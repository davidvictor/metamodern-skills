# The expression sheet

One sheet per candidate, eleven lines, written side by side with its rivals before any credits. The sheet exists because adjectives alone produced interfaces and generic boards; behaviour, lineage, verbs, and colour roles produced distinct worlds.

## The eleven lines

1. **Name.** One word. It is an internal label; the prompt says it never appears on the board. Prefer words that cannot pass as copy.
2. **Thesis.** One plain sentence the organization would recognize as its own.
3. **Tension.** Adjective plus counter-adjective ("exact but never cold", "loud but precise").
4. **Lineage.** Two or three genres the model already knows, as genres: engineering drawings, pit-wall timing graphics, broadsheet front pages, screen-printed gig posters. Never studios, photographers, or competitors; the descriptors go in, the names stay out.
5. **Verbs.** Three to five formal actions the world performs: dimension, section, index; sweep, cut, crop; disperse, drift, condense. Verbs generate form; the model treats them as a pattern grammar. They are also internal labels.
6. **Colour roles.** For the fixed palette: which colour is the field, which is the type and line, which is the signal, which behaves as light or ink; proportion rules. The palette never changes; the roles carry the difference.
7. **Typography behaviour.** Classes, weight contrast, case, scale, what type does at the edge. Never typeface names.
8. **Composition and density.** Grid or editorial, dense or spare, scale contrast, negative space.
9. **Imagery treatment.** How things are drawn or photographed: dimensioned line drawing, duotone halftone, campaign still lit from above.
10. **Imagery subject.** What is drawn or photographed: an offer as a layered system, a service relationship as architecture, or invented people moving through a defined environment. Keep this separate from treatment because the director may keep the treatment while rejecting the subject.
11. **Rejected default and failure line.** The category cliché this expression refuses, and the specific way this expression fails ("reads as an engineering firm", "becomes a car brand"). The failure line is the critique key at Stage 4.

Materiality can ride on line 9 or stand as a twelfth line for print-led brands.

## What makes a set

- Each candidate bets on a different source of authority (precision, momentum, intelligence made visible, tactile authority, the product itself). A set whose candidates only redeploy the palette three ways is a taxonomy, not three worlds.
- A literalism test: a candidate that cannot be described without naming an industry object (a microphone, a car, an ON AIR sign) is an illustration, not an expression.
- The nearest candidate to the current brand is a control; expect it to read as the product or the current site.

## Brief shapes

**Distinct bets.** Three to five sheets, each grounded in a different source of authority.

**Distance scale.** Five to seven sheets from "current brand disciplined" to "a different house", each step changing its source of authority. Keep fixed identity inputs stable and name each permitted variation.

**Matrix.** When the director asks for more options on a known axis, hold everything else and cross the opened axes: four subjects by light and dark by restrained and structural device made sixteen boards whose gate could be asked by axis. Two waves of eight.

## Generic worked sheet: Specification

| Line | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Thesis | A complex service becomes an inspectable system |
| Tension | Exact but never cold |
| Lineage | Engineering drawings, exploded-view diagrams, dimensioned specification sheets, restrained industrial product documentation |
| Verbs | Dimension, section, index, annotate, align |
| Colour roles | White is the field, black is the type and the line, blue only as the dimension line, the callout, and the index mark, never a fill larger than a swatch |
| Typography | Wide engineered grotesque for statements, compact tabular sans for data, numerals and index codes everywhere, small caps labels |
| Composition | Modular, hairline rules, generous white space, visible grid |
| Imagery treatment | Dimensioned engineering line drawing, flat and shadowless |
| Imagery subject | The service as an exploded stack of labeled layers with one decision traced to a practical consequence |
| Rejected default, failure | No dashboard glow, cyber grid, gradients; fails as an engineering firm |

The sheet keeps treatment and subject separate so a critique can change one without reopening the whole direction.
