# Design language and handoff

Stage 6 writes three things from what is locked: the design language, the recipe bank, and the Figma build list. Then Stage 7 files brand facts and method lessons separately.

## Design language, nine lines

Write from the locked board and the accepted expansion tiles, in plain sentences a designer could build from:

1. **Idea.** The thesis in one sentence, plus what the brand draws and traces.
2. **Treatment.** How everything is drawn or photographed; what it never is.
3. **Device.** The recurring form, its parts, its rule (whole, hairline, at scene scale), and any secondary form. Note any device withdrawn at expansion.
4. **Colour roles.** Per mode: field, type and line, signal roles, proportion rules, what never glows or fills.
5. **Type behaviour.** Classes, weight contrast, case, scale; real typefaces are chosen natively.
6. **Composition.** Grid, separators, space, where the drawing sits against the type, what is loudest after the headline.
7. **Copy behaviour.** Headline shape, one figure per surface, the words that keep every heading in the brand's category.
8. **Materiality.** Stock, finish, one accent material.
9. **Failure lines.** The category reads to screen against.

Explain desired behaviour first; add an exclusion only where it prevents a demonstrated failure.

## Recipe bank

A table keyed by purpose: job id, model, references with roles, and a note. Full prompts stay with each job on Higgsfield and in the run ledger. This is what makes the direction reproducible after the images expire.

## Figma build list

Native production belongs to `metamodern-work-in-figma`. List what gets built as editable sources: the lockup from the existing logo files; the device as vectors with its labels as components; a type scale in chosen faces; colour tokens per mode; the accepted applications rebuilt over the generated drawings as image layers or over the vector device; component sets for callouts, dimension lines, or whatever the device needs. Generated boards are never production guidelines.

## Application proofs

Use the smallest set that exposes whether the system works: one expressive surface and one information-bearing surface at least. A hero alone does not prove a report, a website, or a deck. An accepted, editable, verified proof becomes the production foundation.

## Verify handoff

Before Brand System acceptance or artifact production, confirm: the platform traces to the expression; the director's corrections appear in the language; the direction holds under real content; expressive and functional surfaces both hold; type, colour, imagery, layout, and device work together; Figma uses licensed fonts and editable elements; accessibility, reproduction, and licensing are addressed where relevant.

At brand level, return the language and proofs to `metamodern-develop-brand` for Brand System acceptance. At artifact level, record the art direction in project context and editable sources, then return to the parent playbook for production, review, and delivery.

## Brand facts versus method lessons

Brand facts (the lock and its amendments, decisions and their verbatim words, rejected directions, naming used on surfaces, the recipe bank, where images were copied) go to the brand's workspace and `BRAND.md`. Method lessons (what a prompt line did, what a gate shape caught, what a tool substituted) go to the run ledger and, when they survive a distinct case, are proposed for this package.
