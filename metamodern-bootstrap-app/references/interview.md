# Bootstrap interview

Ask only questions whose answers are unavailable and needed now. Reuse answers supplied by the request, existing authority, accepted artifacts, or a composed initiation run. Preserve their source. Group related gaps, recommend a reversible default with its reason, and stop once the selected bootstrap task has sufficient decisions.

## Order

1. **Named outcome and constraints.** What is the immediate task, its acceptance evidence, exclusions, source authority, delivery boundary, and any consequential compatibility, privacy, or spending constraint?
2. **Identity.** What display name is approved or provisional, what brand assets are available, and which technical slug, repository identity, and domain are already fixed? Derive reversible values only when they are not supplied.
3. **Marketing disposition.** Choose one: retain and adapt the in-app marketing site, minimize it to the needed public surface, or use an external marketing site. For an external site, ask for its destination only when a link or redirect is needed by the current task. Derive root, signed-out, and signed-in entry behavior from this choice.
4. **Only applicable account and provider decisions.** Ask about team accounts, authentication methods, social login, billing, email, CMS, or other providers only when the current task or retained surface needs them. Confirm what is local stub behavior versus a real provider obligation.
5. **Targeted starter work and local handoff.** Confirm surfaces to keep, adapt, hide, remove, or defer and the first development task. Derive technical local IDs, ports, callback values, and configuration from the checkout. Ask only when ownership is ambiguous or required user-owned access is absent. Never ask for secret values.

## Decision rules

- A current requirement can preserve an account, auth, billing, or provider boundary even when its setup is deferred.
- Brand assets may be absent. Record that gap and preserve current placeholder assets until an authorized asset replacement is available.
- Do not infer legal names, operators, domains, provider accounts, product copy, or product model details.
- Pause only the step that depends on a missing material decision or capability. Continue read-only inspection and independently authorized preparation.
- Recommend a default only when it is reversible and supported by current evidence. Name its effect, for example retaining marketing while copy and assets are incomplete or deferring a dormant provider integration.
