# Research-led Brand Development Run

## Default path

For development or refresh work without an accepted Brand Platform, use this order:

1. Foundation
2. Audience
3. Position
4. Meaning
5. Character
6. Brand Platform
7. Visual Direction
8. Identity
9. Application Proof

For an established brand, recover and confirm what is already true at each strategy stage rather than treating the brand as blank. Preserve settled equity, develop only material gaps, and revisit an earlier stage when current evidence exposes a real contradiction. An established brand with an accepted Brand Platform may enter at the earliest material gap.

## Operating loop

Use the same research and correction loop at each stage:

1. Read the workspace run state, project evidence, accepted decisions, and relevant prior research.
2. State the decision this stage must produce and the uncertainty research can reduce.
3. Dispatch independent research agents when useful lanes can run in parallel. Use the project's research brief, templates, and output paths when they exist.
4. Reconcile the findings into one coordinator synthesis. Do not hand the designated approver an unfiltered stack of agent reports.
5. Present what the evidence supports, the working interpretation, material tensions, what research cannot determine, and a small set of consequential clarification questions.
6. Let the designated approver correct, refine, or accept the interpretation.
7. Record the evidence, synthesis, designated-approver corrections, stage decisions, and remaining uncertainty in the workspace.
8. Advance only when the stage is coherent enough to guide the next stage.

Research comes before clarification. Do not begin with a blank interview when project or public evidence can support a useful first interpretation. Clarification questions should challenge specific research or synthesis, not ask the designated approver to perform the agent's research.

Each pass belongs to one stage. Do not bundle later-stage research and founder decisions into the current clarification round. Capture useful later-stage observations as leads, then return to them when their stage begins. Bring in later-stage evidence early only when the current stage cannot be understood without it.

Do not infer moral, ethical, legal, compliance, privacy, or data-governance positions from research or operating constraints. Keep them in the systems that govern operations, risk, or claims unless the designated approver or the person defining the brand explicitly chooses one as brand material.

## Stage research contracts

### Foundation

Research the offer, product and business evidence, founder statements, origin, contribution, current role, future ambition, and explicit contradictions. Separate observed facts from founder beliefs and agent inference. Research can recover and interpret founder evidence; the designated approver controls founder intent, purpose, vision, mission, and acceptance.

Foundation may use audience or market context to understand the offer and contribution. It does not select the priority audience, position, visual direction, or application during this stage.

### Audience

Research plausible priority audiences, relevant roles, decision context, problems, desired outcomes, existing behavior, alternatives, and fit boundaries. Use direct customer evidence when authorized and current public or industry evidence where useful. Do not invent personas or select a priority audience only because one product flow is well documented.

### Position

Research direct alternatives, adjacent alternatives, status quo, category conventions, proof styles, and credible differences. Use the competitor and convention audit when a current market refresh can change the answer. A difference is not a position until the brand can support it through conduct or proof.

### Meaning

Trace the offer from practical utility to emotional, social, and broader value. Research consequences, language, norms, and tensions around the problem. Do not manufacture a higher purpose or import moral and ethical positions on the agent's initiative. The designated approver corrects the useful brand belief, change, and values and may explicitly establish moral or ethical material.

### Character

Research founder language, customer expectations, category tone, anti-references, delivery behavior, and credible promise boundaries. Develop plain-language character hypotheses before optional archetypes. Reconcile personality, worldview, promise, voice, and message behavior for designated-approver correction.

### Brand Platform

Reconcile the corrected Foundation, Audience, Position, Meaning, and Character into one coherent platform. Complete the narrative spine, message order, reasons to believe, and usage guidance. Add research only when synthesis exposes a material gap and route that gap to its owning stage.

### Visual Direction

Use the accepted Brand Platform to research the visual field, references, anti-references, cultural and category codes, and expression opportunities. Define distinct territories and generate one coherent 16:9 editorial brand-world board per territory. Each board shows typography, color, graphic language, photography, materiality, digital context, physical application, environmental application, and one unexpected extension. Product screens and individual application mockups belong after direction selection. The designated approver selects, combines, or corrects a direction before Identity.

### Identity

Develop the selected direction into the relevant mark, typography, color, imagery, layout, graphic devices, iconography, and motion. Keep editable sources in Figma. Connect each decision to the Brand Platform and selected direction.

### Application Proof

After Identity, test the system on two or three priority applications that expose both expression and functional use. Verify the editable structure and rendered result. Route strategic, directional, or identity failures back to the stage that owns them.

## Workspace state

Record the current stage, stage status, completed stages, active research lanes, current research synthesis, designated-approver corrections, stage decisions, and next action. Draft prose or a populated strategy field does not mark a stage complete. A stage becomes complete only after research, synthesis, and designated-approver correction produce a coherent basis for the next stage. Record Brand Platform acceptance explicitly in the stage decisions; correction, continued work, or silence is not acceptance.
