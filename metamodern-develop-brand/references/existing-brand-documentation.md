# Existing-brand documentation

Use this when recovering the brand as it exists, not selecting a better direction. The output is one useful, evidence-backed profile or Draft Brand System, not a sequence of new strategic approvals.

## Bind the subject and sources

Resolve the specific brand, parent or sub-brand scope, authorized workspace, and existing source register before research. Related events, products, publications, and initiatives can explain relationships; their audiences, missions, or identities do not automatically belong to the subject brand. Give every delegated lane the subject, mode, relevant sources, and permitted destination, or keep it read-only. Check returned scope before incorporation. Reuse existing project records rather than creating another registry.

## Recover explicit answers before inferring

Use likely source locations to fill real gaps: internal guidelines and editable assets; About and founder material for origin and mission; Careers or culture pages for explicit values; capabilities and contact pages for offer and audience; representative editorial writing for voice; current applications for expression. This is a targeted recovery map, not a required full-site crawl.

An explicit, applicable values list takes precedence over traits inferred from tone. Preserve formal values, broader stated beliefs, and inferred personality as different things. An older founder statement can explain origins without defining every current characteristic. Describe gaps only after checking the relevant available sources; a failed reader or missing search result does not prove that material does not exist. Use another authorized reading surface when useful, without bypassing authentication or access controls.

## Keep evidence specific

For material findings, retain the source, relevant content date, retrieval date, subject/scope, and what was actually inspected. Distinguish an original report or event date from a later upload path or article date. Prefer an explicit date in the artifact; record conflicting dates without silently choosing one. Undated content stays undated.

Keep published brand claims, observed assets, historical accounts, direct customer testimony or work credits, and agent synthesis distinguishable. A logo, event attendee, editorial guest, showcased ad, or partner listing is not interchangeable with evidence of client work. Public work evidence does not establish a current engagement or payment status. A case-study label or published methodology does not independently verify an outcome. Use concise labels where they prevent confusion, not a new scoring system.

## Inspect and describe existing expression

Inspect representative rendered material when visual identity or message hierarchy matters. Text extraction can omit a hero, wordmark, or image-based headline. Do not infer appearance from alt text or treat omitted extracted text as absent on the page. Record the surface and date actually viewed; one desktop screenshot does not establish mobile, print, or motion behavior.

Describe the current hierarchy, distinctive wording, certainty, tone by context, and recurring visual roles. Keep documentation separate from recommendations. A generic category claim may still be part of the existing brand; competitor research should clarify the current meaning only when needed, not launch a search for new positioning or whitespace. Record substantiation concerns alongside the claim instead of rewriting the brand to resolve them. Do not force retain/refine/create decisions in this mode.

## Complete the profile without inventing production rules

Reuse the existing profile or handoff destination; consolidate answers rather than adding a competing document for every research pass. Mark obsolete stage questions or earlier recommendations superseded when the mode changes, while retaining useful evidence and the scope of actual user decisions.

Separate documentation coverage from production readiness and final handoff acceptance. Published derivatives and screenshots can support a profile while editable masters, licenses, or application specifications remain unresolved. Do not invent clearspace, color conversions, type scales, or asset rights from observed use. Ask about a missing production detail only when the requested deliverable needs it; do not block the whole profile.

Before reporting completion, check the requested fields, source links, evidence labels, and saved artifacts. When saving captures, verify they open and their format matches the filename. Re-read changed destinations and preserve concurrent edits. Report local persistence separately from cloud sync or publication. Keep reusable method lessons in the shared source and client facts in the client's authorized systems.
