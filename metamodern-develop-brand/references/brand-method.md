# Metamodern Brand Method

Select the run mode in `SKILL.md` first. For existing-brand documentation, these areas check evidence coverage and describe the current brand; they are not approval stages or instructions to redesign it. The ordered development path below applies when establishing or changing the brand.

For development or refresh work without an accepted Brand Platform, use this ordered path: Foundation, Audience, Position, Meaning, Character, Brand Platform, Visual Direction, Identity, and Application Proof. Research and synthesize at each stage before asking the designated approver for clarification. Record their corrections before advancing. For an established brand, recover and confirm what is already true at each strategy stage, preserve settled equity, and develop only the material gaps.

Adapt the depth and research lanes to the evidence. Combine cards within a stage and revisit an earlier stage when later evidence exposes a contradiction. Do not skip an ungrounded stage merely because draft language or product work exists. An established brand with an accepted Brand Platform may enter at the earliest material gap while preserving settled equity.

## 1. Foundation

Establish the offer, founder intent, contribution, future ambition, and current role. Separate business facts from beliefs and untested claims.

## 2. Audience

Identify the priority audience, decision context, relevant roles, needs, desired outcomes, and fit boundaries. Describe real decisions rather than inventing persona detail.

## 3. Position

Examine direct and indirect alternatives, the status quo, and category habits. Define a credible difference and the proof that can sustain it.

## 4. Meaning

Connect the offer to practical, emotional, social, and broader value. Articulate the useful brand belief, change, and values. Do not invent moral or ethical positions; include them only through explicit founder or owner direction.

## 5. Character

Define recognizable behavior, personality, promise, verbal character, and the message behavior those decisions imply. Use archetypes only as an optional private lens after plain-language territories are clear.

## 6. Brand Platform

Compile the grounded strategy into one coherent foundation. Complete the narrative, message order, supporting proof, and usage guidance. Keep durable brand messages separate from campaign copy, headlines, and calls to action.

This is a synthesis step, not an automatic new discovery phase. Route a material gap back to the stage that owns it.

## 7. Visual Direction

Translate the Brand Platform into principles for expression. Audit existing equity and decide what to retain, refine, or create. Research the reference field, develop meaningful visual territories, and generate one coherent brand-world board per territory. Do not let product screens or other individual applications define the brand direction. Let the designated approver select or correct the direction before Identity. Use `metamodern-explore-brand-expression` for this stage. Otherwise apply the identity method directly.

## 8. Identity

Develop the selected visual and verbal direction into an editable, usable system. Keep source design work in Figma and connect each choice to an established principle.

## 9. Application Proof

After identity development, test the system in the applications most likely to expose weaknesses. Application Proof is a review role, not an early strategy shortcut. An accepted, editable, verified proof can become the production foundation for the applicable downstream capability.
