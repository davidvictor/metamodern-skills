# Workspace Guide

Record the run mode. For existing-brand documentation, track evidence coverage, observed and inferred guidance, unresolved contradictions, preserved choices, and remaining work. Do not require completed discovery stages.

When a user corrects the mode, reconcile the workspace and active profile together: supersede obsolete stage questions or recommendations, retain useful evidence, and record only the decision actually made. A short reply to one question does not approve unrelated choices. Keep the existing profile or handoff as the single synthesis destination.

Update the workspace during the work. Preserve distilled meaning, useful source context, open tensions, accepted decisions, and the next action. Leave routine conversation and full transcripts out by default.

For development or refresh work without an accepted Brand Platform, maintain explicit run state: current stage, stage status, completed stages, active research lanes, research synthesis, designated-approver corrections, stage decisions, and next action. Draft content does not complete a stage. Record completion only after the research and correction loop produces a coherent basis for the next stage.

Where source status could be confused, use plain labels such as `Observed`, `Agent synthesis`, `Designated-approver correction`, `Working idea`, and `Accepted`. Use labels only when they clarify the origin or standing of the material.

Keep client work in its authorized store. The workspace remains `Status: Draft` and is unavailable for external claims, even when it records accepted internal decisions.
