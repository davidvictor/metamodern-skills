# Brand System Output Guide

An existing-brand Draft may present a complete reconstruction supported by evidence, with inference and source limits identified. Documentation does not establish new founder intent or brand decisions. Keep the handoff Draft until explicitly accepted; do not require separate acceptance of every documentation section.

Report documentation coverage, production-source readiness, and acceptance separately. An unresolved editable master or license can limit a particular asset without preventing a sourced profile. Record the source or specification needed for that use; do not invent missing rules or reopen the brand strategy.

Publish only useful accepted sections. Keep discarded exploration and empty fields out.

Include one integrated section for sources, assets, licensing, application proof, and downstream handoff. Link the authoritative editable source, relevant asset locations, license notes, evidence from application proof, and the inputs needed by the next capability.

Write the portable handoff to project-root `BRAND.md`. Keep the file `Status: Draft` until the designated approver explicitly says it is accepted. On acceptance, update `Status` to the project's accepted status term and `Last reviewed` to the acceptance date in the same change. Route material downstream gaps back into the workspace.
