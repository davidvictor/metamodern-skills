# Strategy reconciliation

## Helps resolve

Whether the current strategic picture is coherent enough to guide identity and communication.

## Useful when

Existing brand material is strong but fragmented, or decisions from several conversations need a single practical interpretation.

## Review first

Accepted decisions, workspace findings, customer evidence, delivery realities, and the strategy fields that are already useful.

## Prompt bank

- In one clear sentence, what does this brand help people do and for whom?
- What do clients or customers actually choose it for?
- Which belief guides choices when tradeoffs appear?
- What commitment can delivery consistently support?
- What should the priority audience remember after everything else fades?

## Method

Compare the current proposition, position, belief, promise, and message against evidence. Resolve conflicts where possible and preserve material uncertainty where it remains.

## Synthesis

Write a compact strategic throughline, the decisions it supports, and the questions that still prevent a confident choice.

## Contributes

An aligned basis for identity principles, voice, and the Brand System.

## Cautions

Do not turn a reconciliation into a new discovery program or present a working conclusion as accepted without evidence.
