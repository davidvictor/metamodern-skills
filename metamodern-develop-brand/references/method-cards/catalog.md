# Method Card Catalog

## Implemented pilot cards

- [Existing brand inventory](existing-brand-inventory.md): Preserve settled equity, organize evidence, and find the practical gap.
- [Competitor and convention audit](competitor-and-convention-audit.md): Refresh the alternatives and category habits that could change the current brand decision.
- [Strategy reconciliation](strategy-reconciliation.md): Align current strategic decisions into a usable throughline.
- [Voice and message completion](voice-and-message-completion.md): Complete durable voice and message guidance from existing evidence.
- [Identity system audit](identity-system-audit.md): Decide what identity elements to retain, refine, or create.
- [Color system completion](color-system-completion.md): Give color roles and values the clarity needed for real use.
- [Application proof](application-proof.md): Test the system in priority contexts before downstream production.

## Future roadmap

### Foundation

- Working proposition: Define the relevant value the offer creates for the priority audience.
- Founder intent: Surface the founder perspective that should inform durable choices.
- Purpose, vision, and mission: Distinguish the contribution, future condition, and present role.

### Audience

- Priority audience: Identify the people whose decisions the brand must serve first.
- Problems and desired outcomes: Connect audience needs to the change they seek.
- Buying roles: Clarify the people who influence, approve, or use the offer.
- Fit and working boundaries: State the contexts and relationships where the brand is most useful.

### Position

- Alternatives and category: Map the choices and category expectations the audience compares.
- Differentiation: Identify the difference the organization can credibly make.
- Position and proof: Connect the chosen place in the market to supporting evidence.

### Meaning

- Belief platform: Articulate the conviction that guides the brand's contribution.
- Value and transformation: Relate the offer to practical, emotional, and social change.
- Higher-order value: Test the broader value that makes the brand matter beyond utility.

### Character

- Personality territories: Explore useful behavior-led character directions.
- Worldview and behavior: Define the perspective and observable conduct behind the brand.
- Promise and delivery truth: Align the customer commitment with what delivery can sustain.
- Voice and contextual tone: Describe stable verbal character and appropriate situational shifts.
- Optional archetype lens: Use an archetype only as a private lens after plain-language character is clear.

### Message

- Narrative spine: Shape the story that connects the brand's relevant decisions.
- Message architecture: Order the ideas and proof the audience needs to understand.
- Message and proof testing: Check whether the message remains credible and clear in use.

### Synthesis

- Brand Platform synthesis: Compile useful strategic decisions into one coherent foundation.

Strategy reconciliation is already implemented.

### Identity

- Creative translation: Turn strategy into principles for visual and verbal expression.
- Mark and logo behavior: Define the mark's role and reliable use across contexts.
- Typography: Establish type choices and behavior that support the identity.
- Imagery: Define the image direction that adds relevant meaning.
- Graphic devices and layout: Establish repeatable compositional elements and rules.
- Iconography and motion: Add these expression systems only where they improve use.

### Conditional

- Naming: Resolve a name only when the brand has a real naming decision.
- Brand architecture: Clarify relationships among offers, sub-brands, or parent brands when needed.
- Additional audience segments: Extend the strategy only when another audience materially changes the work.
- Research or validation techniques: Select evidence-gathering methods that reduce a specific uncertainty.
