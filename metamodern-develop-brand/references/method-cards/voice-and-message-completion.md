# Voice and message completion

For existing-brand documentation, recover how the brand currently sounds and orders its messages. Preserve distinctive phrasing, certainty, and contextual tone; separate any recommendation or substantiation concern from that account. Questions about what deserves new emphasis apply to development or refresh, not faithful documentation.

## Helps resolve

How the brand should sound, what it should repeatedly communicate, and where tone may adapt.

## Useful when

Language exists in fragments, the voice is recognizable but undocumented, or priority channels need clearer guidance.

## Review first

Accepted strategy, representative writing, priority-channel examples, customer language, and claims delivery can support.

## Prompt bank

- Which existing lines sound unmistakably like this brand?
- What should the language make people feel and understand?
- Which qualities should stay stable across channels?
- Where should tone change with context?
- Which familiar category phrases feel false, vague, or overused?
- Which three ideas deserve repeated emphasis?

## Method

Collect representative language, identify its durable characteristics, and distinguish foundational messages from temporary headlines or calls to action.

## Synthesis

Describe the stable voice, contextual tone range, message order, and supporting proof in language people can apply.

## Contributes

Voice guidance and a concise message architecture for the Brand System.

## Cautions

Avoid generic personality labels without observable writing behavior. Do not make claims the organization cannot consistently support.
