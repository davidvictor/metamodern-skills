# Competitor and convention audit

## Helps resolve

Which direct alternatives, adjacent alternatives, status-quo choices, and category habits materially affect the current brand position or expression.

## Useful when

The market field is incomplete or time-sensitive, a prior landscape needs a refresh, or the current difference depends on claims and conventions used by others.

## Review first

The current brand workspace, project source register, existing competitor or landscape research, current decision, and any project research brief or output template.

## Prompt bank

- What does the priority audience compare in this decision?
- Which direct, adjacent, and status-quo alternatives can change the current position?
- What has changed or was missing since the last point-in-time review?
- Which naming, verbal, visual, proof, and experience conventions are saturated?
- Which apparent whitespace is credible enough to support with conduct or proof?

## Method

Define the decision the research must improve. Scope only the independent lanes needed to reduce that uncertainty, such as direct alternatives, adjacent categories, or category expression. Use a project research brief and output location when they already exist. Parallel research is optional when the lanes do not share state.

Start from the existing landscape and report the delta. Preserve prior point-in-time evidence rather than silently replacing it. Prefer current first-party sources and record retrieval dates. Distinguish fresh rendered observation, first-party page claims, search-indexed text, independently verified facts, and inference. State when a visual review could not be refreshed.

Compare the field across buyer, decision, offer, proof style, naming, tone, visible expression, and operating model only where each dimension helps the decision. Separate a published claim from demonstrated performance, adoption, data rights, legal clearance, or customer truth.

## Synthesis

Name what changed, what merely confirms the prior view, the saturated conventions, credible whitespace, anti-imitation guidance, and the uncertainty that still requires customer, product, legal, or visual validation. Return the smallest update to the brand hypothesis or next decision.

## Contributes

A current alternatives field, category-convention view, and evidence-backed input to differentiation, position, naming, voice, or visual direction.

## Cautions

Do not turn public copy into market truth, collect competitors without a decision purpose, infer legal rights from public use, or treat a search result as fresh rendered evidence. Keep project facts in the project workspace rather than in the shared method.
