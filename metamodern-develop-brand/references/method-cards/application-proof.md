# Application proof

## Helps resolve

Whether the chosen strategy and identity work together in the few touchpoints that matter most.

## Useful when

A system needs practical evidence before a Brand System is completed or a downstream capability begins.

## Review first

Priority touchpoints, accepted strategy, current identity guidance, editable source files, and the intended decision.

## Prompt bank

- Which two or three touchpoints matter most to the current decision?
- What must remain stable across them?
- What should flex with context?
- Which system gap becomes visible only in application?
- Is the problem in the brand system or in this individual artifact?

## Method

Create the smallest set of proofs that can expose the current decision. Compare them for consistency, appropriate flexibility, and any gap that prevents reliable use. Treat application proof as a review role. When a proof is editable, structurally sound, verified, and accepted, use it as a production foundation rather than recreating it.

## Synthesis

Capture what held, what failed, and whether the next action belongs in the system or the individual artifact.

## Contributes

Application evidence and focused revisions for the Brand System.

## Cautions

Keep reusable website, deck, and document systems in their downstream capabilities. Continue from an accepted proof only when its structure and editability are suitable for production.

## Provenance

Independently written Metamodern pilot card informed by the approved capability design and internal brand evidence.
