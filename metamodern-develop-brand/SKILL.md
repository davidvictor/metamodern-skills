# Metamodern Develop Brand

## Choose the requested outcome

Use `documentation` to recover and codify an existing brand, `audit` to assess an existing system and report substantiated gaps, and `development` or `refresh` to establish or change strategy or identity choices. An audit does not begin guided discovery, change the Brand Platform, generate visual territories, or create a new `BRAND.md` unless the user asks to act on its findings. Preparation of accepted inputs for a website, deck, document, or proposal uses the accepted Brand System and reports missing inputs; its production belongs to the owning capability.

Reuse accepted workspace decisions, `BRAND.md`, source records, and completed proofs. Resume at the earliest material gap. When a proposed change invalidates an accepted platform, identity decision, or proof, identify the dependent work and return to its owning stage for a new decision rather than treating the old acceptance as current.

## Continue documentation, development, or refresh

For documentation, development, or refresh selected above, choose from the requested outcome separately from brand maturity or documentation status. Use existing-brand documentation to recover or codify the brand as it exists. Use development or refresh to establish or change strategic or identity choices. A missing accepted Brand Platform does not by itself turn documentation into development. An audit stays in the bounded assessment route unless the user asks to act on its findings.

For existing-brand documentation, research current guidance, assets, published language, and behavior. Use the strategy fields and identity areas as coverage prompts, not sequential approval gates. Infer the current system where evidence supports it; distinguish published material, observed expression, synthesis, and unresolved gaps. Preserve existing claims and voice while recording substantiation limits separately. Existing explicit founder or brand statements can establish what the brand says; do not manufacture values or intent from operating constraints. Inspect existing expression without waiting for platform acceptance. Do not invent new ambition, positioning, values, or visual territories. Continue across the documentation without per-stage questions; ask only about a material unresolved contradiction or a substantive change requiring the designated approver's choice.

The guided discovery, strategy-development, visual-exploration, and application-proof sequence below applies to development or refresh. Documentation follows the evidence-recovery path above. Source authority, project isolation, final handoff acceptance, and external-action boundaries apply in both modes.

For documentation, read [existing-brand documentation](references/existing-brand-documentation.md) for source recovery, evidence classification, preservation, and completion checks before assembling the profile.

## Establish context

Locate the authorized workspace. Read its current state, accepted brand material, and source links. Read the project's authority documents and integration guidance when they exist. An Agency checkout may add Agency-specific authority documents and adapters. Preserve source boundaries. Use only canonical material carrying the project's approval status for external claims. Treat accepted decisions in a Draft workspace as internal until approval.

When the named project is outside the current checkout, resolve that target before doing brand work. Read its local instructions, project brief, source register, and working-tree state, then derive brand artifact paths from those records. Do not create a second workspace or assume the current checkout owns the project.

## Start or resume

Classify brand maturity as new, partial, or established. Separately record the run mode as documentation, development, or refresh, and determine whether an accepted Brand Platform exists. Compare the workspace, `BRAND.md`, current project truth, settled equity, current expression, and available proofs; surface material contradictions before synthesis. Keep Figma read-only during this inventory.

For a new or partial brand without an accepted Brand Platform, use this ordered path: Foundation, Audience, Position, Meaning, Character, Brand Platform, Visual Direction, Identity, and Application Proof. For an established brand in development or refresh mode without an accepted Brand Platform, use the same ordered grounding proportionately: recover and confirm what is already true at each strategy stage, preserve settled equity, and develop only the material gaps. Draft fields, research, product work, copy, and visual proofs are inputs; their presence does not complete a stage. An established brand with an accepted Brand Platform may resume at the earliest material gap.

## Guide discovery

Guide the designated approver as the default facilitator. At each stage, inspect project evidence and conduct the useful research before asking them to supply answers. Use independent research agents when distinct evidence lanes can run in parallel. Reconcile their work into one sourced synthesis, then present findings, a working interpretation, tensions, and a small set of consequential clarifications. The designated approver corrects, refines, or accepts the synthesis. Record those corrections in the workspace before advancing.

Keep the pass focused on the current stage. Do not dispatch later-stage research or ask the designated approver to decide later stages in the same pass unless that evidence is necessary to understand the current stage. Preserve useful later-stage observations as leads for the stage that owns them.

Research may propose founder intent, meaning, values, ambition, character, or promise from available evidence. It cannot establish those founder-led decisions on the designated approver's behalf. Keep full transcripts out unless the user asks to preserve one. Direct client interviewing is an explicit mode.

Do not infer moral, ethical, legal, compliance, privacy, or data-governance positions from research or operating constraints. Include one in brand purpose, meaning, values, character, or messaging only when the designated approver or person defining the brand explicitly establishes it as brand material. Do not turn an operating requirement into a brand virtue on the agent's initiative.

## Synthesize strategy

Complete Foundation, Audience, Position, Meaning, and Character in order. Reconcile useful evidence, open tensions, the designated approver's corrections, and accepted decisions into the Brand Platform, then present the integrated platform for acceptance. Keep working material in the workspace and distinguish observed facts, agent synthesis, the designated approver's view, working ideas, and accepted decisions where needed. Do not begin Visual Direction until the Brand Platform is accepted.

## Translate into identity

After Brand Platform acceptance, reconcile the early equity inventory against the accepted strategy and decide what to retain, refine, or create before moving into Visual Direction. Keep Figma read-only during preflight. At brand level, generate one coherent editorial brand-world board per territory. Do not use product screens, website sections, slides, dashboards, or other single applications as the primary brand-level probes. Use `metamodern-explore-brand-expression` for expression sheets, boards, critique, selection, refinement, expansion, and design language. Let the designated approver select or correct the direction before developing the editable identity. Otherwise apply the identity method directly so Brand Development remains independently useful.

## Prove the system

After identity development, test the system in the priority applications that expose weaknesses or clarify the final system. Application Proof is a review role, not an early strategy shortcut. When a proof is editable, structurally sound, verified, and accepted, use it as the production foundation for the relevant downstream capability. Reusable website, deck, and document systems remain owned by those capabilities.

## Accept and hand off

Use `BRAND.md` as the project-local Brand System handoff. Keep it Draft until the designated approver explicitly accepts it. On acceptance, update its metadata and integrated source and handoff section together. Hand off accepted inputs while routing material gaps back to the workspace.
